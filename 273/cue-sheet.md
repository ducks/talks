# 273″ cue sheet

A three-movement presentation after John Cage's *4′33″*.

Do not ask the audience for silence. Their movement, breathing, laughter,
typing, hallway noise, HVAC, and discomfort are the content of the talk.

## Before going on

- Ask the host to introduce the talk by title so you do not need to speak.
- Put `talk.md` on the projected display and a stopwatch on the private display.
- Disable notifications, screen blanking, and audible timer alerts.
- Begin with slide I already prepared but not yet projected.

## Performance

| Elapsed | Action | Movement length |
|---------|--------|-----------------|
| `0:00` | Project slide I and start the stopwatch together. | `0:30` |
| `0:30` | Advance to slide II. | `2:23` |
| `2:53` | Advance to slide III. | `1:40` |
| `4:33` | Quit the deck, close the laptop, and leave. | — |

Do not speak between movements. The slide transitions are the movement
boundaries. Do not explain the piece at the end; let the program description
and the experience do that work.

The three durations follow the commonly performed timings documented by the
John Cage Trust: 30 seconds, 2 minutes 23 seconds, and 1 minute 40 seconds.

## Recovery

If a transition is a second or two late, keep the final endpoint at `4:33`.
The total duration is the part the title promises.

If the projector fails, continue the timed performance without it. If someone
speaks or leaves, continue. Neither event interrupts the piece.
